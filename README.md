# HeritageLens AI
## Rediscover the Forgotten Stories of Our Past

HeritageLens AI is an intelligent heritage discovery platform designed to bring overlooked cultural and historical sites into the digital spotlight.

Across towns, villages, and hidden landscapes, countless heritage locations remain invisible despite their historical significance. Ancient temples, forgotten forts, sacred lakes, stepwells, battlegrounds, and vernacular architecture often lack digital presence, storytelling, and public awareness.

## Our platform transforms these forgotten places into immersive digital experiences through:

- cinematic storytelling
- interactive exploration
- heritage discovery
- visual narratives
- community-driven cultural preservation
- What We Aim To Solve

## Many culturally significant heritage sites:

- receive little to no visitors
- lack online discoverability
- have disappearing oral histories
- are absent from mainstream tourism platforms
- remain undocumented for younger generations

HeritageLens AI bridges this gap by creating living digital identities for these sites.
